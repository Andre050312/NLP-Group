Neutral scope pairs — human review

80 sentences, 40 pairs. Read the instructions before starting, then work alone.
How to judge each pair
  1. Target is neutral. The highlighted target aspect is only mentioned; the sentence expresses no opinion about it.
  2. Only the distractor changed. The two sentences differ only in the opinion word about the other aspect, and the meaning of the target is unchanged.
  3. Natural English. Both sentences read like something a reviewer could plausibly write.
  4. Distractor polarity. In the positive sentence the other aspect is clearly positive, in the negative sentence clearly negative.
Approve only if all four hold. If one fails, reject and say why in the notes. Do not look at any model output while judging.
